# Sellers — who delivered and who didn't

Track record on the people and shops Carson actually buys from. Feedback scores are a
starting point; this file is what happened *to us*.

**This file records facts, not behavior changes.** If a pattern here becomes a reason to
change how Quint works — "always confirm rotation with this vendor before ordering" — that
is a Learning Loop candidate for [`lessons.md`](lessons.md), promoted through the gate.

## What to record, and why

- **Fitment accuracy above all.** A seller who ships fast and packs well but describes parts
  loosely is more dangerous to this project than a slow one who is precise. Rare marine parts
  are frequently listed by people clearing out an estate or a shop, who genuinely do not know
  what they have.
- **Whether they answer questions before purchase.** A seller who will read a casting number
  off the part for you is worth a premium — that is Tier 1 identity evidence you cannot get
  any other way.
- **Packing on heavy iron.** Manifolds, covers, and pumps arrive damaged more often than
  people expect.
- **Whether the return actually happened.** A stated policy and an honored one are different
  facts.
