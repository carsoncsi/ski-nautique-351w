## Checked and rejected — do not re-download

**PCM Owner's Operation and Maintenance Manual, part `L510010-13`, printed 03/13**
`https://pcmengines.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/imagesL510010-13.pdf` — 86 pages, 1.6 MB,
downloadable without login.

**WRONG ERA. Do not use for this boat.** It states its own coverage as 2013 model year only:
`EX343 · HO303 · ZR409 · ZR450 · ZR490 · XS550 · XR550` — all GM-based. Whitespace-tolerant
text search of the full extracted layer returns **zero** occurrences of `351`, `Windsor`, or
`5.8L`.

This is a textbook F1 — an official manufacturer document, from the manufacturer's own domain,
that looks exactly right and covers the wrong application.

**Two things did fall out of it, both labeled:**

1. **Transmission fluid — Dexron III ATF.** PCM's own text: *"All PCM, Velvet Drive and Hurth
   transmissions — Use Dexron III automatic transmission fluid."* Covers PCM-branded gears as a
   family. `[canonical source, but 2013 edition — PROBABLE for a 1994 PCM 40-series, not CONFIRMED]`
2. **A "TRANSMISSION IDENTIFICATION PLATE" exists** and is item 3 in PCM's engine-identification
   figure, alongside the engine specification decal (1) and engine identification decal (2).
   The figure is captioned *"(5.0/5.7L)"* so the position won't transfer to a 351, but it
   confirms PCM does fit a distinct transmission ID plate near the engine. Partially chips at
   the "no documented tag location" gap in [`../351w-swap.md`](../351w-swap.md).

**Also 403 to automated fetch** (live in a browser, Carson can get them logged in):
`correctcraftfan.com/reference/manuals.asp` · `correctcraftfan.com/downloads/PCM_Owners_Manual_Ford_and_Chevy.pdf`
· `manualzz.com/doc/11485284/pcm-302-351-engine-service-manual`
